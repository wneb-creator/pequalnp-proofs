## Frontier analysis — lemma_route

The exactly-two-junk argument does not localize merely by choosing two live dirty roots for each positive edge. The proof needs the lower dirty root's operands to be OR-only and the upper dirty root's operands to be raw OR inputs plus that one lower root. With additional dirty ancestors, replacing the frontier roots by virtual inputs loses precisely their behavior on independent assignments; the resulting functions need not be inputs of a graph function with the same girth. Restricted to one positive pair, every wire has only five possible monotone types (0, x, y, x-or-y, x-and-y), but collapsing that trace preserves neither negative assignments nor the forcing certificates used in the neighborhood-cover proof.

There is also a charging obstruction: each local two-root cover yields at best a few centers times the entire graph support. Summing separate covers multiplies by the number of root pairs. An actual extension needs either disjoint support-entry budgets, a shared representative family, or a proved global consequence of leastness. No such amortization was established in this focused pass. The fixed-depth representative-family extension is already implemented by structure_proof; it should not be duplicated.

### Explicit nonleast family showing unbounded bad forcing certificates

Let G be the matching with edges a_i b_i for 1<=i<=m, m>=2. Let F be its graph function. For each choice of one variable from each matching edge, form the OR clause C_sigma of those m variables. The conjunction of all 2^m clauses is exactly F. Arrange the all-b clause B=(OR_i b_i) last, and let D be the conjunction of the other clauses. Then

    D = F OR (AND_i a_i),     F = D AND B.

Proof of the first identity: if F is false, each pair has a zero variable. If any a_i is zero, select it and one zero from every other pair; this gives a false clause other than B, so D is false. Conversely, when every a_i is true, every clause other than B contains a true a_i. Thus the only independent assignment making D true is the full a-transversal. Its minimum independent forcing certificate has size m.

Computing each clause by an OR chain and conjoining them by an AND chain uses m*2^m-1 gates. Every proper AND prefix is dirty, witnessed by the a-transversal; only the final AND is clean, so the circuit is TopForm. Its final dirty root has empty raw orIn, hence starEdges is empty and Ustar contains all m matching edges. All circuit inputs are essential to F, all gates lie in the output cone, and each AND-chain operand is semantically necessary locally. The graph has no cycles. Nevertheless the circuit is explicitly nonleast: the usual DNF uses 2m-1 gates.

finite_search fully checked m=3 and m=4: 23/63 gates, minimum bad certificates 3/4, exact semantics and TopForm. The m=3 distinguished dirty root has only one bad assignment, {0,2,4}. These are obstruction controls, not refutations of L1735 and not evidence of least circuits with such witnesses.

Correction to an abandoned shortcut: omitting one maximal-independent-set complement clause in an arbitrary graph need not yield F OR AND(S). Proper subsets of S may have no other maximal independent extension. For a general maximal independent S, the desired D=F OR AND(S) can instead be represented by all negative-input complement clauses of D, but that may require many additional clauses and supplies no size lower bound.
