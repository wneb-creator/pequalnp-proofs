# Literature read 2026-11-25 UTC

Primary paper: Stasys Jukna, Disproving the Single Level Conjecture, https://web.vu.lt/mif/s.jukna/ftp/single1.pdf (opened/read via web tool).
Quote: “We therefore need entirely new lower bound arguments”.
The paper gives quadratic Boolean functions with a large separation between unrestricted monotone circuits and single-level circuits. Those constructed examples are saturated extensions, not incidence graphs of planes, so they do not refute this gap. Its Problem8.2 asks for a superlinear lower bound for an explicit quadratic graph function. This is historical context, not a claim that the problem remains unresolved as of2026.

Primary paper: Stasys Jukna, Notes on Boolean Read-k and Multilinear Circuits, https://web.vu.lt/mif/s.jukna/ftp/readk-submit.pdf (opened/read via web tool).
Quote: “Read-1 circuits have the same power as monotone arithmetic”.
Theorem1 identifies minimum read1 size for homogeneous multilinear support with monotone arithmetic size. General read2/read1 separations do not specialize to the projective-plane function.

Our inference from the checked local corpus: the assigned claim would establish the exact unrestricted monotone bound N(q+2)-1 for incidence graphs, by combining the existing READ1 lower bound and star upper bound. Merely removing repeated variables from proof trees is not a justified size-preserving circuit transformation. No external figure is being used as a Lean premise.
