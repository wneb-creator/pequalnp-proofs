Potential unimplemented next pruning observation, recorded at pause: a gate whose value is false on every accepted edge assignment can be replaced by false in a monotone circuit. This preserves all target minterms and can only decrease the output, hence preserves the graph function. A minimal-circuit compiler could therefore rule out such gates even when they are not below the target. This needs a formal deletion/constant-propagation proof and has not been used in the recorded search.
