# P4 symbolic trace semantics

The two referenced Lean modules compile. ReductionsIR.lean defines a typed five-gate candidate, one-hot source variables, symbolic Tseitin values, and a total CNF assignment. ReductionsSemantics.lean proves that its encoded output equals a direct five-gate AND/OR evaluator on every four-input assignment, and that its two encoded derivative outputs equal the explicit formal derivative recurrence. All three semantic theorems use only propext and Quot.sound.

These are genuine correspondence lemmas, but the complete clause-satisfaction bridge is still in progress. This archive does not yet establish a five-gate lower bound, and does not prove L-meta-2602. The 49-clause shape sample also compiled; like the earlier 30-clause prototype, its final goal is True and it is only a compilation checkpoint.

The generators assume the job scratch directory shown in their source. Restore the parent repair_nearmiss_v3.py and and_count_search.py dependencies to that directory/PYTHONPATH, and decompress the original audit-path-center5.cnf from kernel_lrat_control there. Running p4_bridge_generate.py reconstructs the symbolic trace and checks byte equality with that CNF. Running p4_bridge_lean.py --count 30 --split produces the IR module, and p4_semantics_generate.py produces the semantics module. Their source and dependency hashes are recorded. Import the preserved Reductions module first; compile these standalone research modules with their directory on LEAN_PATH under the project's lake environment. They are research artifacts, not the final Outside PR format.
