L-meta-1024 SAT search normalizations

These are computational search encodings. A solver UNSAT verdict or LRAT trace
alone is NOT a Lean proof of the posted lower bound. The full circuit-to-CNF
translation and certificate reconstruction would still require formal checking.
All final SAT candidates are independently scalar-tested on all 3003 required
inputs before being called counterexamples.

Domain and orientation
  Original rows: thirteen-bit inputs of Hamming weight seven or eight.
  Output w: OR of points {w,w+1,w+3,w+9}, modulo thirteen.
  Dual rows: Hamming weight five or six, with AND of each line as target.
  Gates use two distinct predecessor indices, op=true AND and op=false OR.

Output sinks and padding
  Two different original target zero regions on the band are disjoint. Replace
  outgoing uses of an earlier target output by true; when that output is false,
  every other target is true, and monotonicity preserves the latter's truth.
  Constant-propagate, delete copy and dead gates, then place output sinks last.
  To reach exactly 38 live gates from fewer, replace one sink f_L by OR(f_L,p)
  for p in L. This preserves band correctness and makes the old sink internal.
  Choose p != 0 when preserving a low fanout for input 0. The dual padding is AND.
  Thus every internal gate may be required to feed a later gate, and output
  gates may be forbidden as sources. The directed graph then ends at outputs.

Ordering and point symmetry
  Among ready internal gates, greedy Kahn ordering chooses the smallest key
  (maximum predecessor, minimum predecessor, operation), OR before AND. Any
  newly ready gate reads the just-created wire, so its maximum predecessor is
  larger than the previous selected maximum. Weak lexicographic order is sound.
  The 5616 explicit projective-plane permutations were checked against the exact
  thirteen Singer lines. point-pair-maps.json supplies every ordered-pair map to
  (0,1). After removing constants and copies, the first gate has two inputs, so
  unrestricted search may fix its pair to (0,1), with operation left free.

Low input fanout partition
  For s live gates and 13 distinct output sinks, at least s-13 predecessor edges
  start at gates. At most s+13 <= 51 edges start at the thirteen inputs, so some
  input has fanout at most three. Map it to input 0; live padding avoids input 0.
  Priority first-layer pair cases are: (0,1) if any pair contains 0; otherwise
  (1,2) if any pair is noncollinear with 0; otherwise (1,3). These have minimum
  maximum predecessor 1,2,3 in their respective remaining classes. All 1014
  center/pair permutations are in fanout-stabilizer-maps.json and were audited.
  All THREE cases must be UNSAT for an exhaustive computational partition.

Syntactic support and operation count
  Each line input is essential on the band: use six zeros consisting of its line
  plus two outside points, then flip that line point to obtain five zeros.
  Exact syntactic reachability is the union of both predecessor supports, so
  every output must syntactically reach its four line inputs.
  Support.circuit_support_exact also proves that any band-correct output with
  global input support <=7 is globally ordinary. The future normal-strong query
  therefore guards an at-least-eight syntactic-input-support constraint by each
  exact nonordinary flag. It requires a true support bit outside every excluded
  seven-point subset. Running earlier queries are not changed.
  The checked OR-only lower bound excludes zero AND gates. A separately reviewed
  mathematical single-AND normalization excludes one AND. OneAndWork.lean later
  compiled the full <=1 actual-AND lower bound, including nullary/unary gates and
  invalid/future wire reads. Strong runs therefore
  require at least two AND gates (at least two OR gates in dual orientation).

Redundant boundary rows and global ordinary outputs
  At complement(L), every other output is forced true; own L is free.
  At complement(L union M), outputs L and M are forced false; other outputs free.
  At singleton p, outputs whose lines exclude p are forced false; others free.
  These give 104 distinct input rows (13+78+13). Values follow from monotonicity
  and suitable seven/eight-point band extensions or restrictions.
  A globally ordinary output equals OR(L) on ALL inputs exactly when its own
  complement(L) value is false and all four line-singleton values are true.
  Nonordinary is the disjunction of own-complement true and any missing singleton
  positive. It is NOT equivalent to just own-complement true.
  OneExotic.lean was independently checked: with at least twelve globally ordinary
  outputs, every band-correct circuit has >=39 gates. Strong runs therefore require
  at least two nonordinary outputs. The subsequently compiled and independently
  reviewed TwoExotic.lean proves that eleven ordinary outputs already force >=39,
  so the launched strong runs require at least THREE nonordinary outputs.
  The count is encoded by requiring a true flag outside every excluded pair.
  ThreeExotic.lean subsequently compiled and passed final independent review:
  ten ordinary outputs already force >=39. The unrestricted all-3003-row run
  launched at 02:59:51 UTC therefore requires at least FOUR nonordinary outputs,
  using a true flag outside every excluded triple. Existing fanout runs retain
  their minimum of three and their encoding-source snapshots.
  ExoticWitnessExtra.lean then compiled and passed review, proving the exact
  five-literal ordinary-output iff and the >=4 cardinality condition on these
  precise flags. The mathematical-to-flag characterization is thus Lean checked.
  Strong-normal runs still allow missing singleton
  positives even though all own-complement values are false.

Normal dual subclass
  If each dual output is true on its exact four-point line, it equals AND(line)
  on all supports of size <=6. It suffices to constrain all 1716 six-point inputs
  and the thirteen line inputs. An OR output root is redundant: a child true on
  the line is already true on every line superset and below the correct output.
  Thus normal dual roots may be forced AND. Any resulting witness is converted
  by swapping AND/OR, then checked on all 3003 ORIGINAL band inputs.

Local symbolic search
  Fixed outputs are direct three-gate OR trees. Every line offers six pair and
  four triple OR signals. Used signals must fit one of its fifteen direct trees:
  two disjoint pairs, or a pair contained in a triple. The compatibility graph
  allows no larger mutually compatible family. Reconstructing chosen trees gives
  an actual network, checked independently after full bitset validation.
  The finished three-output searches cover both projective triple orbits and
  all 15^10 direct-tree choices for the other ten outputs. They are computational
  local UNSAT results, not an unrestricted lower bound.
