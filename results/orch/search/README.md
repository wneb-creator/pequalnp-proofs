# Pilot 176 finite two-junk circuit searches

These programs and logs are computational evidence for L-meta-5012. They do not constitute Lean proofs and do not establish the universal statement.

`antecedent_search.py` enumerates fan-in-two AND/OR circuits through an SMT encoding using complete truth-table bitvectors and the installed Z3 C library. `twojunk_critical.py` uses only edge-pair assignments (required true) and maximal independent-set assignments (required false). Monotonicity makes those critical rows sufficient for an edge function. Every SAT witness reported by the critical-row program is separately re-evaluated on every Boolean input before being saved. The earlier full-row program constrains all input rows in SMT but does not perform this separate post-check.

The encoding restricts attention to forms necessary for a minimum circuit: no gate signature repeats an earlier gate or input; all gates are used; each commutative gate has ordered distinct operands; independent adjacent gates are ordered by their largest operand. Equal critical signatures can be identified while preserving all critical output values, hence the full edge function by monotonicity. Thus a least circuit cannot contain such a repeat. For pure cycles, automorphisms additionally normalize an initial input gate.

The seven graphs in `antecedent-graphs6.json` exhaust nonisolated bipartite graphs with a specified 3+3 partition up to partition permutations and exchange, subject to no four-cycle. The star-cover upper bound is edges + minimum vertex cover size - 1. Searches were run at all budgets from 5 through this bound. Budgets below 5 cannot depend on all six essential inputs. All these searched two-junk instances were UNSAT. P4 at 4 gates and P5 at 5 gates were also UNSAT. A zero-junk P4 circuit at 4 gates was a successful SAT control. A Lean-checked explicit C6 upper-bound witness is separately stored in ../lemmas/antecedent_cycle_six.lean.

The first version asked Z3 for a model even after UNSAT or timeout, so some early logs end with a model-unavailable error after the solver status. Later versions avoid this; the solver statuses must not be confused with a Lean check. UNKNOWN denotes timeout and supports no conclusion. Complete stdout and stderr are retained.

The larger C8 and theta(3,3,3) critical-row searches are recorded separately with exact metadata and timeout. Files are snapshots; root may refresh them after active jobs finish.

Later completed runs: C8 with 11 gates/two junk and theta(3,3,3) with 12 gates/two junk both returned UNKNOWN after 600 seconds. C6 with one attached leaf at 9 gates/two junk returned UNKNOWN after 180 seconds, both without and with the optional edge-support pruning. Those runs support no exclusion claim. The optional pruning requires every gate to be true on some positive edge input and false on some negative maximal-independent-set input; this follows informally from replacing a signal by false/true and propagating it away in a least circuit, but that pruning argument is not yet Lean-checked.

`twojunk_product_matching.py` enumerates input-OR products and verifies the 21 finite certificates later checked by Lean in `antecedent_five_independent.lean`. On C6 it found 41622 clean products of two dirty products, with accepted-edge matching number at most 2. `twojunk_common_filter.py` examines the different same-side pattern B AND (g OR h). It found an 11-gate circuit for C6 whose two junk gates feed a common clean top accepting 3 disjoint edges. This circuit is Lean-checked in `antecedent_cycle_six_two_junk.lean`, including proof that it is not least via the 8-gate witness. It is not a counterexample to L-meta-5012.

The newer `twojunk_normalized.py` adds TopForm using exact gate-ancestor bitsets: no clean AND may occur in the strict cone of any AND. It also optionally requires at least one junk gate to have a nonempty `wmax`, computed on negative maximal-independent-set rows. Positive edge-support and negative-support pruning are now separate flags. The normalized long runs enable only positive edge-support pruning, which the proof agents report established in `restriction_edge_hit.lean`; the optional negative-support flag stays off. The root agent supplies the checked normalization and both-empty-wmax exclusion needed to justify these necessary conditions for the target case.

Encoding controls: the fixed non-TopForm four-gate P4 circuit is SAT without TopForm and UNSAT with it; the checked eleven-gate C6 two-junk circuit is SAT with TopForm, some-junk-cleaner, and positive-edge-support constraints. SAT models are separately re-evaluated on all inputs, and ancestry/wmax conditions are separately recomputed from the concrete model. `geometric_clean_star_oracle.py` tests a proposed strengthening, not the posted gap. It enumerates bipartite C4-free graphs and incomparable nonempty OR-arm pairs, checks full truth tables, and searches for two dirty products whose conjunction is below the graph function, neither globally lowerable to a clean star using its original other arm, and not both disjoint two-cleaner flags. The connected/wide-arm run exhausts vertex counts2 and3 before finding a counterexample on P4 at4vertices: edges02,03,12; products `(0 OR1) AND(0 OR2)` and `(0 OR1) AND(2 OR3)`. Both accept and guard all edges, and their conjunction equals the graph function. The natural6gate circuit is not least (a4gate circuit suffices). Therefore this refutes only the geometry without leastness. Removing the clean-covered middle edge02 still leaves a matching, while removing an end edge allows a residual star. The run stops at the first counterexample and is not a census for larger vertex counts. The exact P4 facts, including nonleastness, are kernel-checked in `../lemmas/antecedent_star_dichotomy_counterexample.lean`.

A second geometric run also requires each product to have disjoint arms. It exhausts vertex counts2 through4 for the both-guarded/common-edge category, then finds a connected P5 counterexample at5vertices: edges02,04,12,13; g=(0 OR1) AND(2 OR3), h=(0 OR1 OR3) AND(2 OR4). Their conjunction is below F; each has guarded edges, and their guarded-edge union is all graph edges. Neither full accepted-edge set admits the specified original-arm clean-star replacement, while h is not a flag. Exact wmax sets are {1,2,4} and {0}; g has distinguished flag centers1,2 plus an external cleaner4. This is finite computation, not yet a separate Lean certificate, and does not assert leastness.

`geometric_guarded_matching_oracle.py` tests another proposed shortcut: with singleton-overlap g and a nonempty internal cleaner, if dirty h jointly cleans g and accepts two disjoint g-guarded graph edges, must g AND one original h OR-arm be clean? Full truth-table enumeration refutes this at5vertices: P5 edges02,04,12,13; g=(0 OR1) AND(0 OR2); h=(1 OR4) AND(2 OR3 OR4). Shared guarded matching04,12; the two proposed triple products are false on independent assignments01 and03. Removing raw-clean-covered edge13 leaves a possible Ustar subset entirely g-guarded. This is a geometric obstruction, not a least-circuit result. The run completed n2 through4 and stopped at this n5 witness; it is not an n6 census.
`geometric-triple-factor-counterexample.json` is a separately full-truth-table checked C6 example with a nonempty mutual residual23: g=(0 OR1 OR3) AND(0 OR2), h=(1 OR2 OR4) AND(3 OR5), exact cleaner sets{1,5}/{0,4}. It refutes raw triple cleanliness outside the guarded-only case; no claim of leastness or Lean certification is made.

`geometric_union_cover_oracle.py` asks whether the union of all graph edges accepted by two jointly clean dirty pure products has a vertex cover of size3 under singleton-overlap/internal-cleaner/nonempty-residual hypotheses. The C8 run exhausted384 eligible pairs with no4matching; theta333 stopped at a4matching witness after18 eligible pairs. Its matching uses an h-only unguarded edge, so it does NOT refute an actual-Ustar3-cover claim. `geometric_ustar_cover_oracle.py` corrects the tested edge set to guard(g) union guard(h) union common(g,h), an admissible superset for Ustar. It exhausted384 C8 and3648 theta333 eligible pairs with no4matching. Both use all wide incomparable OR-arm pairs over8vertices, full256-row cleanliness, exact cleaner sets, and additionally require r absent both h arms. These are finite tests on two named graphs, not a general theorem or Lean proof.
