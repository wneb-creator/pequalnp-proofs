# One-NOT input-OR witness experiments

These are bounded heuristic searches for an ordinary 19-gate Petersen circuit whose unique NOT negates an OR of two original inputs. Each start has the physical OR/NOT prefix (2 gates), all ten input roots, the complemented OR, and optionally the positive OR; the positive suffix budget is 17. Edge and nonedge source pairs are both explored. Counterexample independently reviewed the emitter and both positive controls on all 1,024 rows. The controls have 22 and 21 physical gates and are correctly rejected at cap 19.

No 19-gate candidate was found. The baseline OR-edge retained search completed its heuristic tree; all other runs reached their ordinary time limits. These outcomes are NOT UNSAT certificates. The full-row variant additionally checks the necessary componentwise row order condition for arbitrary positive AND/OR continuations. Its refinement and the original adapter have independent source reviews in review-notor-adapter.txt and review-notor-full-cover.txt under results/orch.

Every complete stdout/stderr, command, exit status, supervisor, exact source, source delta, matrix snapshot and control circuit is preserved. Executables are reproducible with g++ -O2 -std=c++17. Supervisors retain original scratch paths and CPU/resource limits; paths must be adapted for a new environment. No external memo is used, and negative seeds are not falsely listed as positive projected-history gates. Only a fully reconstructed, independently truth-table-checked candidate could establish a witness; the search emits no accepted candidate here.
