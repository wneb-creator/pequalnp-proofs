Scope: exploratory finite computation, not a Lean proof or a theorem for Petersen.

Exact AND-count enumeration flattens every OR-only region into an arbitrary OR of original inputs and earlier AND outputs. Therefore, for a fixed count k of binary AND gates, every AND input belongs to that finite OR closure; the final output is an OR of supplied generator functions. The enumeration checks whether the OR of all supplied functions contained in the target equals the target. After a new AND it checks the same condition. This preserves arbitrary sharing and allows unbounded OR-gate cost. Constants can be removed for the nonconstant monotone targets without increasing AND count. The dual-C5 search uses f*(x)=not f(not x), exchanging AND/OR counts.

Complete computational results: C5 has no at-most2-AND circuit; C6 likewise. DualC5 has no at-most3-AND circuit, giving computational evidence for C5's OR count at least4. K2,2 is a positive control with its one-AND factorization. Source and complete output are preserved. No SAT/UNSAT claim is promoted to Lean; the separate generic SAT attempt on C5/C6/C7 reached only UNKNOWN at its12-second limits, with a fully truth-checked4-gate path positive control.

Structural obstacle: ((a OR b) AND (c OR d)) AND (a OR c) computes ac OR ad OR bc. The first AND produces a nonstar biclique including the unwanted pair bd, and the final AND filters it. Hence assigning each graph edge to its first producing AND does not produce a star cover even for a triangle/C4-free path. A correct global lower bound must charge later filtering and control reused OR gates. R_meta_5375_41 already isolates a local-repair hypothesis for this normalization step; L_meta_8545 supplies only the matching star-profile upper construction. Neither establishes the needed lower bound.

Sufficient remaining statements would be ANDcount≥vertexCoverNum and ORcount≥edgeCount−1 for arbitrary circuits computing girth≥5 graph functions. These are not claimed proved here. The small tests found no counterexample; they do not establish the general assertions or cost-preserving normalization.

Additional bounded free-OR SAT: an encoding with only the costly AND gates represents each operand as an arbitrary OR of inputs and earlier AND outputs. C5 with3AND is SAT, with its model checked on every input; C5 with2AND is UNSAT in0.001s and C7 with3AND is UNSAT in0.184s. Petersen with5AND reached UNKNOWN after25seconds. These solver outcomes are computational observations, without a Lean proof or a separately checked UNSAT certificate.
