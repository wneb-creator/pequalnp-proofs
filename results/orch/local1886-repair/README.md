The bank-substitution script exhausts all15 Petersen edges, both supplied AND/OR operations, and all18 gate-deletion choices. All consumers of the deleted gate are redirected to the free bank. None of the540 variants preserves the near-witness function; the best17-gate replacement has21 truth-table errors. All1024 inputs are evaluated. The40 best variants are retained as data.

For a realizable choice of four pair-OR prefixes, computing K in two new gates would retain an18-counted-gate candidate after adding the supplied edge bank. On these six variables, D AND K must equal the three-edge matching {05,23,69}; the other four center inputs can all be false.
