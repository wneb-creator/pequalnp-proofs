# L-meta-9811 work in progress

This branch contains checked partial results and research artifacts. It does not
prove or refute the posted claim, and no proof PR has been opened.

The posted claim excludes every AND/OR/NOT circuit of at most 24 gates for the
14-input, 19-edge graph function in the desk statement. Reductions are closed
for this step. The named proof file currently establishes a lower bound of 20
and an upper bound of 25. Its build passes; the official checker rejects it
with `no-theorem` because the exact posted theorem is absent. The complete
outputs are in `named-build.txt`, `axioms.txt`, and `checker-posted.txt`.

## Checked components

- `lemmas/AndSevenRootFormat.lean`: ordinary monotone circuits computing the
  target need at least seven AND gates. This does not constrain arbitrary
  circuits containing NOT gates.
- `lemmas/GraphProfile.lean`: finite kernel certificates for the target graph,
  including exact uncovered-edge minima and five/six/seven-center profiles.
- `lemmas/BlockingAll.lean`: local sole-reader restrictions valid even with
  arbitrary NOT gates elsewhere. Exact SAT-facing conditions are recorded in
  `lemma_search/BLOCKING-CUTS.md`; they are not a new global size bound.
- `lemmas/ChainRectangle.lean`: in a specified monotone TopForm circuit with
  exactly two dirty AND gates, no centreless top, and at most one extra OR,
  the later dirty gate has a rectangular set of true bichromatic input pairs.
  The circuit assumptions remain explicit.
- `lemmas/TopSide.lean` and `lemmas/CommonEntry.lean`: exact primary-or-dirty
  semantics for top inputs, and a common nonempty primary support.
- `lemmas/OneExtraOR.lean`: the completed general structural result. For a
  trimmed AO2 TopForm circuit computing a bipartite C4-free graph function of
  maximum degree three, zero centreless tops and at most one extra OR imply
  `Ustar.card ≤ 2`. There is no restriction on the number of dirty AND gates.
  Independent recompilation and an axiom probe passed; only `propext`,
  `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` occur. This remains a conditional
  monotone-circuit theorem, not the posted unrestricted lower bound.
- `lemmas/RectangularTop.lean`: a rectangle intersecting a clean star in a
  C4-free graph of maximum degree three has at most two off-center edges.
  Its final wrappers count actual unordered edge sets.
- `lemmas/StarOutside.lean`: the corresponding incident-edge counting lemma
  when a common endpoint has already been identified.

These are compiled helper artifacts, not substitutes for the posted theorem.
Most longer files contain copied prefixes so each component can be checked
independently. Their exact hashes, scope reviews, and full compiler outputs
are preserved beside them. The edge-tracing and rectangle-count connection is
complete in `CriticalCase.lean` and then generalized in `OneExtraOR.lean`.
Other monotone cleanup regimes and the unrestricted-NOT case remain open.

The general one-extra-OR argument works as follows. Uniqueness of the extra OR
forces the relevant dirty signals to have a common entry. Before that entry,
AND gates combine pure monochromatic OR clauses, so their true bichromatic
pairs form a rectangle. The clean top inputs supply a common primary vertex
`z` and a partner set `B` contained in its neighborhood. Every uncovered edge
activates the dirty rectangle, meets `B`, and avoids `z`. C4-freeness confines
these edges to one opposite endpoint; that endpoint already has neighbor `z`,
so maximum degree three leaves at most two uncovered edges.

## Searches and certificate route

`official_sat/` contains a kernel-only LRAT smoke proof, generators, a decoder,
and an independently audited bridge to the repository's certified encoding.
The relevant theorem is `PEqualNP.Library.LRAT.LinkN.size_of_unsat2`, defined
in `LinkN2.lean`. Python and Lean generated byte-identical target CNFs with
1,466,230 clauses over 571 assignments: all 552 negative inputs and all 19
minimal positive inputs. A kernel-checked LRAT proof for that exact formula would suffice;
a satisfying model still needs validation on all 16,384 inputs.

The official 240-second, 1,200-second, and final 300-second phase-guided runs
all returned UNKNOWN. Their partial LRAT traces are not certificates. The
general reader-cut and capacity-cut searches also returned UNKNOWN. The custom
SAT encoders and completed earlier searches are preserved in `counterexample/`
and `counterexample_phase/`. Restricted UNSAT results do not imply the full
24-gate claim. The official encoding remains unchanged by heuristic cuts.

`abc_search/` records 10,631 fully validated mapped outputs; the best has
25 gates. Valid 27-gate circuits containing one NOT are search seeds only;
their existence is neither a refutation nor evidence that NOT helps.
`star_cut/` and `small_one_not/` contain bounded computational searches with
their exact frozen-prefix or finite-graph scope recorded explicitly. The final
used/unused aggregate split, in `final_retry_pair_39/` and
`final_unused_pair_39/`, completes exclusion of all 42 tested pair families.
This does not classify all circuits. The 26 small-graph cases likewise provide
only finite computational evidence. The final deletion enumeration in
`counterexample_deletions/` enumerated 1,324,800 candidates and evaluated the
1,061,015 with an output-reachable NOT; no witness was found.
All searches have stopped.

## Remaining obstacles

Ordinary restriction recurrences reached 20. Even an optimistic recurrence
using hypothetical sharp cofactor bounds stopped at 23, so repeating that
recurrence alone does not close the gap. No applicable size-preserving
NOT-elimination theorem was found for this graph. Formula, rank, and external
normalization approaches examined so far do not provide the required bound;
see `formula-research.md` and `official_sat/normalization-research.md`.

The named file has 9,249 lines and 522,141 bytes. A completed submission must
replace or extend it with the exact final theorem, pass the checker, and have
an upstream PR diff containing only that one allowed proof file. Research
artifacts can remain accessible in this branch's earlier commits.
