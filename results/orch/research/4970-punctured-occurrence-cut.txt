Punctured-Fano optional raw-input occurrence cut
Source: merged lean/PEqualNP/Outside/L_meta_910.lean, mergeInput lines351-394 and free_bound lines396-416. The source specializes n=14 but its merge argument does not use Fano incidence; adapt only the finite family of maximal independent sets.
For any collapsed free-OR AND circuit computing a graph function and vertexv, let Free(v) be gates whose two raw-input sets both omitv. Associate each maximal independent set I containingv with the left-operand truth values on Free(v). If distinct I,J have the same signature, choose w in I\J. Maximality gives acceptance on J+{w}. Induction across AND gates transports this acceptance to I orJ: at gates containingv one operand is already true on both; at remaining gates equal left truth bits prevent a crossing. Both I,J mustreject, contradiction. Thus #MIS_containing(v)<=2^|Free(v)|. This counts containing gates, not literal occurrences; duplicate AND barriers remain valid.
For the independently enumerated punctured graph, MIS memberships in input order0..11 are [11,7,11,7,7,7,7,7,7,11,7,11]. With exactly5AND gates, input vertices0,2,9,11 may be present in either operand of at most1gate; other inputs may be present in at most2gates. This is a sound mathematical consequence of the checked generic argument, but has not been independently Lean-specialized here. A separate SAT wrapper may impose it after review; it alone does not establish AND>=6 or the total bound.
