# Global diagonal-rank-two 3-flat charts

These three polynomial systems cover every three-dimensional complex linear space of symmetric4×4 matrices whose diagonal projection has rank two and whose permanent is constant24 after translation by the all-ones matrix. They are generated systems, not a claim that their solution sets are empty.

Every 3-dimensional flat of diagonal projection rank 2 has a 1-dimensional hollow kernel. Choose an independent pair of diagonal coordinate restrictions and use S4 to take the unordered pair to {0,1}; choose V,W with those diagonal restrictions equal to I2. Choose a nonzero edge of a hollow kernel vector H. The stabilizer S2 x S2 of {0,1} has the three edge orbits 01, 23, and the four cross edges; normalize its representative to 1, compensating a 0/1 swap by swapping V,W. Subtract the pivot-edge multiples of H from V,W, making their pivot entries 0. The tangent equation sum_all_entries=0 eliminates one other off-diagonal entry of each basis vector. All remaining entries are exactly the 16 free parameters. Conversely the displayed three matrices are always independent by their two diagonal pivots and the hollow edge pivot, and their diagonal image has rank 2. Their first permanent coefficients vanish identically; the 31 degree 2/3/4 equations are therefore equivalent to constant permanent 24 on their span.

The diagonal entries are V=(1,0,x0,x1), W=(0,1,x2,x3), H=(0,0,0,0). For each chart, choose the pivot edge01,23,or02. Set the V and W pivot entries to0 and the H pivot entry to1. Among the other edges, the lexicographically last edge is eliminated by the tangent equation. The remaining four edges, in lexicographic order, receive x4…x7 for V, x8…x11 for W, and x12…x15 for H. The eliminated entries are respectively −(1+x0+x1)/2−sum(V free edges), −(1+x2+x3)/2−sum(W free edges), and −1−sum(H free edges). No inequality or denominator guard is needed.

The generator expands the24 permutation terms of permanent(2J+s·2V+t·2W+u·2H), using integer sparse polynomials. Dividing by16 gives the desired permanent. The constant is24 and all first-order coefficients cancel identically. The six quadratic, ten cubic, and fifteen quartic coefficients give31 equations. Each equation is primitive-integral normalized; its exact rational scale relative to the actual permanent coefficient is stored in the JSON.

The edge-orbit check explicitly enumerates the four permutations of the stabilizer S2×S2. The three orbit sizes are1,1,4. A separate16-subset Ryser evaluation on exact rational matrices verifies all chart polynomials at eight parameter assignments and twelve spectral points per chart, for288 exact checks. The checks also verify symmetry, tangent constraints, diagonal pivots, hollow diagonal entries, and edge pivots.
