# Complete algebraic exclusion of the B-rank-zero stratum

This is a restricted result toward L-gct-231. It excludes trace-zero constant-permanent two-planes whose Hadamard diagonal projection B is zero and whose D projection has dimension two. It is not a proof or refutation of the unrestricted three-dimensional statement. The B-rank-one stratum and the unrestricted extension problem remain open. This argument and its certificates have not yet been formalized in Lean.

Write T'=Q T Q with the normalized four-by-four Hadamard matrix Q=H/2. Set B=(T'11,T'22,T'33), x=(T'01,T'02,T'03), f=(T'23,T'13,T'12), D=x+f, P=3x+f. In this stratum B=0. Let r=P-2D and U=r+D. The homogeneous cubic permanent coefficient is

    U0 U1 U2 - 2 r0 r1 r2.

If none of the r factors is the zero binary linear form, unique factorization in C[s,t] pairs the three U factors with the three r factors: U_i=lambda_i r_sigma(i), with product(lambda)=2. Consequently D=(diag(lambda)*sigma-I)r. Since D has rank two, r has rank two as well. Simultaneous permutation of the three Hadamard axes reduces sigma to identity, a transposition, or a three-cycle. The centralizer orbits of independent r-coordinate pairs have sizes giving respectively one, two, and one charts. The independent audit enumerates all eighteen permutation/pair combinations and verifies this four-chart cover.

For each chart, normalize the chosen pair of r forms to s,t and write the third as a*s+b*t. The five variables are a,b and the three lambda values. The nine generators are product(lambda)-2 and the three quadratic plus five quartic permanent coefficients. All cubic coefficients are verified multiples of product(lambda)-2. Each exact rational certificate is a polynomial combination of these nine generators equal to a nonzero integer. No nonvanishing guard was needed in these certificates; their extra degenerate parameter points are excluded too.

If an r factor is zero, the cubic identity forces some U factor to be zero. Simultaneous axis permutation leaves two cases. For the same index, r0=U0=0 implies D0=0, and D-rank two normalizes D1,D2 to s,t. For different indices, choose r0=U1=0, so r1=-D1; one of the three D-coordinate pairs is independent and gives the three listed boundary charts. These four charts contain all further factor degeneracies. Their eight generators are quadratic coefficients and coefficients of the quartic after subtracting explicit multiples of the quadratic. The exact identity relating that reduced quartic to the actual permanent is included and independently verified.

All eight charts have nonzero-constant rational certificates. The four generic certificates were produced by Singular liftstd and independently multiplied against their original generators over QQ. The child independently reconstructed all generic input generators and checked every certificate. Conversely, the boundary certificates and their coordinate maps were independently rechecked by the parent using a separate Ryser permanent expansion. Complete native inputs, stdout, stderr, and reproducible verification scripts are preserved. Earlier resource-limited eight-variable probes are inconclusive diagnostics; the verified small-chart certificates establish the restricted result.
