The input-NOT case can potentially be strengthened using L_meta_5367: its generic restriction compiler charges every effective reader of either the positive input or its negated wire. Substituting the existing unrestricted sixteen-gate lower bound for the nine-variable cofactor into its arithmetic would give sixteen plus the number of such readers as a suffix-size lower bound. A nineteen-gate candidate beginning with input NOT would therefore have at most two subsequent literal readers. This strengthening has not been compiled or integrated.

The difficult two-reader configuration is a positive reader OR and a negative reader AND. Restricting the input v to false removes the NOT and aliases both readers, saving exactly three gates and leaving a sixteen-gate monotone cofactor candidate. Other configurations can create further constant propagation, but dependencies between the reader gates need explicit treatment.

Naive replacement of the NOT output by a constant is not sound, even for triangle/C4-free quadratic targets. Let p=v OR w and q=(NOT v) OR w. Then (r AND p AND q) OR (s AND u AND (p OR q)) OR (t AND p) equals rw OR su OR tv OR tw. There are exactly two direct readers of the two v polarities, every variable is essential, and the target graph is a four-vertex path plus a separate edge. Replacing NOT v by either constant changes the output. This example does admit a shorter elimination via p AND q=w and p OR q=true, so it only refutes naive constant substitution, not a general size-preserving elimination theorem.

For Petersen, the positive-reader OR has a useful necessary condition. If its other operand g is independent of v, and g(A)=true, then saturating that positive reader forces the v=false circuit output to dominate the v=true output on row A. Thus on an independent set A of the graph with v deleted, g(A)=true requires A to avoid all three neighbors of v and to dominate all of them. Petersen's six nonneighbors induce C6, each attached to exactly one neighbor of v. The only independent possibilities are the two alternating triples of that C6. In particular g is false on every singleton and every independent pair. For v=0 the exceptional triples are {2,8,9} and {3,6,7}. This argument is mathematical, not Lean checked. It needs a carefully stated dependency condition if g itself depends on the negative reader.

This necessary condition does not force g to be false on actual edge-pairs; g may mix useful edges with exceptional independent triples. status_check found checked deletion machinery in R_meta_2590_932 and L_meta_2668: a monotone intermediate false on every true edge-pair can be deleted. It would remove a pure independent triple, but does not remove such mixed wires. L_meta_4849 explicitly refutes a universal no-garbage AND-gate normal form for triangle/C4-free minimal monotone circuits. Therefore a proof may not assume that every optimal monotone circuit is already a clean star construction. L_meta_7241 and L_meta_8933 only prove star-like bounds under additional sharing restrictions.
