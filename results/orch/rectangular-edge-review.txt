intersection B, and a unequal to z. Hence (a,b) belongs to (I.erase z) times
(J intersection B), and e belongs to its Sym2 image. Cardinality of U is at most
the image cardinality, which is at most the product cardinality. No injectivity
or ordered-pair equality is assumed. The already reviewed product bound <=2
therefore applies exactly.

of every member of I=false exclude a in B, since B consists of neighbors of z.
The stated edge witness meets B, so its b endpoint must lie in B. The havoid
hypothesis says z is absent from each unordered edge and therefore implies
its a endpoint is not z. These are precisely the previous lemma's premises.
No conclusion that arbitrary circuit edge sets satisfy the rectangle or
avoidance hypotheses is claimed.
