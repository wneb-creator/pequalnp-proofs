Restricted-search cuts for the dual K4-incidence target, 10 free-OR AND gates.

The dual target is !F(!x), the vertex-cover predicate. It rejects every singleton. L_meta_6784 removes every AND gate directly reading a variable set T when that set itself is rejected, producing the cofactor and reducing AND count by at least the union of those readers. Converting the free-OR dual cofactor back to an ordinary monotone primal circuit gives OR count at most the remaining AND count.

For T={p}, p one of the four K4 vertices, the primal cofactor deletes p. The residual graph has nine edges and is unicyclic (possibly padded by isolated variables). It inherits no4cycles from K4-incidence. L_meta_599 therefore gives at least eight OR gates, hence p has at most two directly reading AND gates.

For T={l1,l2}, two of the six edge-vertices, the target rejects T since two edge-vertices do not cover the graph. Deletion leaves eight edges and a connected unicyclic graph; again no4cycles. Thus the remaining OR count is at least seven, and the union of directly reading AND gates for l1,l2 has size at most three. The capped CNF counts a variable as read by a gate if either side includes that bare variable, so repeated presence on both sides counts once. No unproved singleton stripping or syntactic side-disjointness is imposed. These mathematical cuts have not been formalized together in Lean in this job. Any UNSAT verdict for the capped model is to be reported as restricted-search evidence, not as a checked circuit lower bound.

Additional clique-bare normalization: if an AND operand ORs bare variables containing a nonedge pair a,b, it is true on every maximal false input V minus an edge (the only two false variables cannot be both a,b). Replace that AND gate by its other OR operand. This preserves every maximal-false evaluation and only increases all other evaluations; therefore all positive inputs remain accepted and all negative inputs remain rejected by monotonicity. In the free-OR model, substitution flattens the other operand into every later use and deletes the AND gate. Repeat until every bare operand set is a clique, then pad with unused false AND gates to the fixed budget. The target rejects every singleton, so no direct-variable final-output summands can remain in any correct normalized circuit.
