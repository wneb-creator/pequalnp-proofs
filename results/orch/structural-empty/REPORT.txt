Bounded I=empty structural investigation (counterexample agent, 2026-11-25)

Outcome: no complete Petersen contradiction or cost-preserving formula conversion established. Two useful mathematical restrictions were identified, and unrestricted quadratic extraction was falsified computationally. No large Lean port was attempted.

1. Carrier reconvergence exchange (mathematical argument, independently sanity-checked by status_review; not Lean formalized).
Choose a globally gate-minimal monotone circuit among hypothetical <=19gate counterexamples, normalize strict/live, and consider its I=empty equality case. All ten surplus units are exactly the distinct reserved units: each first pure carrier b_i has fanout2, and every other wire has fanout1. For each singleton label i, carrier flow gives e_i+M_i=2, with e_i>=1.
If M_i=1, its unique carrier branch splits into two chains reconverging once. Before the reconvergence every carrier gate is OR: an AND still carrying i would itself have both inputs carrying i and be an earlier merge. All arm wires have one reader. A side wire cannot depend on an arm wire without introducing another reader; both readers of b_i are already the two arms. Thus the arms may be collected as b_i OR H and b_i OR K without dependency cycles. If the merge is OR, flattening saves one gate. If it is AND, distributivity gives (b_i OR H) AND (b_i OR K) = b_i OR (H AND K). For nonempty arms containing r,s OR gates, the original r+s+1 gates become (r-1)+(s-1)+1+1=r+s; an empty arm is handled by absorption. Renormalize afterward if needed. This contradicts global gate-minimality. It is not a contradiction from StrictLive alone or from minimality restricted to the I=empty class.
Hence a globally minimal equality-case candidate should have M_i=0 for every i. Every gate then has disjoint singleton supports on its operands, and every AND output has empty singleton support. This is not full functional purity or syntactic multilinearity; higher-degree terms remain possible.

2. Exact independent contamination restriction (mathematical domination argument plus exhaustive finite graph verification).
Let b_i be the chosen first carrier, which dominates input i. Suppose b_i is true on an independent assignment S omitting i. For any z adjacent to i, if S union {z} were independent, monotonicity would make b_i true both before and after adding i. Domination would equate the output on the independent set S union {z} with the output after adding i, which contains edge {i,z}; contradiction. Therefore every neighbor z must be blocked by S.
Exhaustive Petersen enumeration shows exactly two such independent S for each i, both triples. For i=0 they are {3,6,7} and {2,8,9}; adjoining i gives one of the five maximum independent4sets. In particular, b_i cannot contain an independent quadratic payload. It may still carry arbitrary graph-edge payloads, and these two independent triple exceptions prevent blindly replacing it by x_i or moving all of its side payload to the final OR.

3. Quadratic extraction is not generally free, even for a C4-free quadratic graph.
Use variables a,b,c,d,e,f. The8leaf monotone formula
  (a OR b OR c) AND (d OR ((a OR e) AND (b OR f)))
has quadratic part
  ab OR ad OR af OR bd OR be OR cd.
An exhaustive truth-table dynamic program enumerating all formulas through8leaves found no <=8leaf formula for that quadratic function. A9leaf formula is
  d(a OR b OR c) OR a(b OR f) OR be.
Direct evaluation on all64 assignments confirms the input formula differs from its quadratic part only at {c,e,f}. The quadratic graph has triangle {a,b,d} and no4cycle, so this is not a counterexample to a Petersen-specific triangle-free extraction theorem.
The exact dynamic program enumerated7579 nonconstant monotone functions on5variables and tested extraction only for those false on every singleton assignment and having a nonzero quadratic part; no increase was found in that tested subset. The6variable search enumerated890014 functions representable through10leaves and found no increase in the tested subset with no true singleton assignment and a nonzero triangle-free+C4-free quadratic part. Those finite searches are evidence only; their UNSAT/minimality conclusions have not been Lean-certified.

Remaining bottleneck.
The first compound carrier chain can indeed be OR-only and its side payload unshared. If that payload H_i is pointwise <= the target edge function, the conditional exchange to a final output OR is valid at no extra cost. What is missing is a valid way to remove independent triple contamination while preserving all needed quadratic payload edges without increasing the formula/gate count. The disjoint-singleton-support normal form may help prove a triangle-free-specific extraction statement, but this investigation did not establish it. No general star normal form or <=20leaf formula conclusion is asserted.
