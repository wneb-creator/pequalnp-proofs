For a graph on Fin(2*m) containing every matching edge (i,m+i), every vertex
cover has at least m vertices. Any cover of cardinality m is therefore minimum.
The proof uses an injective choice of one covered endpoint per matching edge;
it does not enumerate vertex subsets and applies in particular to m=21.
This is a conditional supporting theorem, not a proof of L-meta-2962.
