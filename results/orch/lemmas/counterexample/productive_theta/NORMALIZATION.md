Additional necessary minimum-circuit normalization: every gate accepts at least one positive edge minterm. A gate accepting none may be set false, preserving all edge-minterm outputs and decreasing output everywhere; monotonicity therefore preserves exactly the graph function. False propagation removes the gate without adding gates, contradicting minimality. Thus existence at size at most11 implies existence satisfying this normalization. This mathematical normalization was independently reviewed, but its strict deletion lemma is not yet Lean checked in this task.
