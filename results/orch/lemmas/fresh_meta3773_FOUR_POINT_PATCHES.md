# Four-point patches for L-meta-3773

Start with the 64-gate all-point-star construction. A four-row subformula costs 19 gates. The following results concern replacing those four rows while reusing pure-line ORs from the remaining stars. No pure-point OR spanning two selected points is supplied. A restriction sets the remaining point inputs to zero. Private-line deletions use the separating-literal argument in `fresh_meta3773_LOCAL_STAR_REPAIR.md`.

* If exactly three selected points are collinear, their common line and their three lines to the fourth point induce the theta graph formed by three paths of length three. Each pair of these four lines intersects at a selected point, so outside-star ORs restrict to singletons. Deleting seven private lines leaves a proposed 18-gate patch with at most 11 gates for the theta graph. In `lean/PEqualNP/Outside/L_meta_615.lean`, `theta_exact` gives a lower bound of 12 for this graph by taking `p=q=r=2` and minimum vertex cover size four. The four-vertex cover and a four-edge matching witness this cover size.
* If no three selected points are collinear, the residual incidence graph is the subdivision of K₄. Removing four private lines reduces the candidate budget to 14. The three outside diagonal points provide the ORs of the three pairs of opposite edge-line inputs. `lean/PEqualNP/Outside/L_meta_723.lean` proves the unaugmented graph needs 15 gates. The problem with those extra inputs remains separate.
* If all four selected points are collinear, retain their common line and one private line per point. Eight private lines can be deleted. The residual graph has four point variables, a common line and four private lines; its edges are the eight incidences of each point with those two lines. A proposed 18-gate patch would yield at most ten gates for this graph. Grant every OR of private lines for free. This relaxation covers every useful external input since an outside star cannot meet a selected point's common line and private line together. The exact finite calculation below excludes ten gates in this model. The direct formula uses eleven.

## Four-arm finite calculation

An essential point or line literal must occur as a primitive leaf. The separating-literal argument forces all nine. Use the eight minimal positives and one negative rectangle for each of the 16 point subsets. A connected binary circuit with `g` gates and `l` distinct primitive leaves has `g+1−l` excess fanout occurrences, counted over all its wires except its output. Thus at most two occurrences beyond the nine mandatory leaves can pay for extra leaf functions or repeated reads.

With no shared internal wire, enumerate the read-once formulas on the nine literals and two extra leaf occurrences. The search permits constants and dropping unused leaves. The extras may be repeated original literals or any private-line OR. There are 32 orbits under permutation of the four arms. For each binary partition of the available leaf tokens, an AND root must certify all required positives on both children and divide the negative obligations between them. An OR root has the dual condition. A leaf set can separate a positive from a negative exactly when it contains a feature true on the former and false on the latter. The recursive search enumerates every feasible assignment of the obligations and rejects all 32 orbits.

For one shared internal wire, its input cone is a formula on a subset `S` of primitive leaf tokens. Its output is a monotone Boolean feature `g(S)` subsequently read twice. The leaf tokens are the nine literals together with at most one additional occurrence from the seven types listed in the result JSON, including the empty choice. For any positive-negative row pair which the complement of `S` cannot separate, `g` must be one on the positive and zero on the negative. If the features in `S` cannot meet these simultaneous requirements, the partition can be dropped. For each surviving partition, either enumerate the exact read-once truth tables inside `S` and test the outer formula with two copies of this table, or enumerate the outer formula with both occurrences set symbolically to 0 and 1 and test the induced inner obligations using the same read-once solver. The 119 viable token partitions yield no solution.

If there are two shared internal wires, or one internal wire read three times, all nine literal wires have single uses and no external feature is available. The earliest shared internal wire has an input subtree on a proper subset `S` of at least two literals. Every input in `S` reaches the output only through this subtree. If it used every literal, every remaining gate would use a function of that single bit and could be removed from a minimum circuit. The same separating-row check has zero possible proper partitions on the nine literals, independently of what later gates compute.

The full truth-table calculation and its outputs are in `fresh_meta3773_claw4_fanout.py`, `fresh-meta3773-claw4-fanout.log` and `fresh-meta3773-claw4-fanout-result.json`. It finished in about 1.5 seconds. This diagnostic has not been transferred to Lean. The existing Lean lower bounds cited above are for the ordinary six-cycle, theta and K₄-subdivision functions, not for augmented-input local circuits.
