Independent review of partial artifacts, 2026-11-25 UTC

These are preserved research components, not a completed proof of L-meta-2592.

TerminalOutput.lean is Defs-only and includes the reviewed TerminalNot, TerminalTwo, and TerminalStructural bodies unchanged. The isolated extension fragments preserve the development stages, with each stage's complete stdout/stderr and recorded exit 0. Its final result extracts a monotone circuit at least two gates smaller when the output is a NOT anywhere in a circuit with at most two NOTs computing f with f(bottom)=false and f(top)=true. It handles empty NOT input lists via the library's default-false read semantics, arbitrary ignored second inputs, and gates after the output.

EndpointConstantNotFragment adds semantic endpoint lemmas: a sole NOT directly feeding a final AND or OR must supply the neutral constant, so the other operand computes f. This alone does not construct a smaller circuit or establish a size bound. Its complete source is reconstructed by inserting the fragment immediately before TerminalOutput's final namespace end; its complete compile outputs and exit 0 are preserved.

MonoPublic.lean imports the named partial only as a development test. The floor18 theorem is conditional on a monotone nine-input floor16 hypothesis; this file does not prove that hypothesis. MonoPublicFragment is its exact body without the import. Both compilation streams were empty, and the producing agent reported exit 0.

OrAndCached contains TwoConstants, OrAnd, PairOrAnd, and OrAndBound fragments verbatim after its development import. The retained AND output and a retained positive input both become false under one vertex restriction. The resulting tail bound is 17 binary gates, so an actual two-gate OR/AND prefix has at least 19 binary gates in total. This is not a universal 20-gate lower bound. Independent Python evaluation checked all 720 distinct-input and 90 same-pair row witnesses against the explicit Petersen graph and input permutation. All checks passed. Both cached and individual certificate compile streams are preserved; warnings are only a deprecated tactic.

SecondAndCached gives the weaker tail bound16 for the signed positive-operand cases; its two warnings concern an unused hypothesis. CentralOrBound and CentralOrCircuit give the reviewed centered-OR prefix bounds. CircuitPrefixesLight is a flattened Defs-only development test with nineFloor15 as an explicit hypothesis, avoiding duplicate certificate elaboration. Its wrapper accepts 10 raw inputs plus three supplied centered OR values and bounds only the remaining circuit. The leaves must be pairwise nonadjacent and one must neighbor the center. Its full successful outputs are preserved, including benign warnings. No unchecked assertion is an axiom.
