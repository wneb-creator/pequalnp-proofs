# Smaller residual with two conditional point taps

Use inputs A0,A1,A2,B0,B1,B2,X00,X01,X02,X10,X11,X12,X20,X21,X22.
Let H = OR over i,j of Xij AND (Ai OR Bj). Let A_sum=OR_i Ai and B_sum=OR_j Bj.

A circuit with at most24 gates and three available output wires h,a,b suffices if h=H everywhere and, only when H=false, a=A_sum and b=B_sum. The tap values are unconstrained when H=true. Appending U AND a, V AND b, and two OR gates gives H OR (U AND a) OR (V AND b), exactly the k6_p3 target in28 gates. These conditional tap requirements are necessary for this completion: on H=false, choosing U=true,V=false isolates a, and choosing U=false,V=true isolates b.

The frozen pack is k6_grid_taps.json, generated by generate_grid_taps.py. Tests use targets [h,a,b], with -1 meaning no constraint. It contains76 union assignments: the18 minimal positive edges,50 maximal H-negative assignments, the six point singletons, and the tap-negative maxima (eight with A allfalse, eight with B allfalse; overlap is deduplicated). For a monotone tap, truth at each A singleton forces truth whenever A_sum=true. Falsity on the eight H-negative maxima with A allfalse forces falsity on every H-negative assignment with A_sum=false. The B argument is identical. Thus the finite care pack is complete for monotone circuits.

A27-gate baseline computes H with23 gates and the two point ORs with four more. It was checked against all32768 input assignments. Its31-gate completion was checked on all76 complete k6 positive/negative basis tests. This baseline is over budget; no24-gate residual or28-gate full witness is claimed.

Input map to actual k6_p3 order: [0,1,3,2,4,5,7,9,13,16,12,8,15,14,10]. The two hub lines are U=input6 and V=input11. No search lane was launched.
