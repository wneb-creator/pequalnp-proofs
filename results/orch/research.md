The counterexamples in Theorem2.1 use saturated extensions: each bipartition side becomes a complete graph. They therefore do not directly supply a C4-free witness for this step. The paper distinguishes computing the full graph edge function from merely agreeing on pair inputs; our search requires the full function. No claim about the modern status of the general bound follows from this2006 paper.
