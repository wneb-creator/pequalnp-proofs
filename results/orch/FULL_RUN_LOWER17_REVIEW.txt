Exact hypothesis and conclusion
fullRun_lower17_of_deleted_lower assumes H: for every natural p and every bank of
Boolean functions on nine inputs, Reach true (retRaw 9) p bank together with
Available true bank structuralDeletedEdge implies 15 <= p. This is a lower bound
in the free-NOT Reach model: constants and complements of stored wires are available
without additional gates, and p counts constructed binary AND/OR operations.
A monotone-only lower bound, or a lower bound charging NOT gates, would not establish
this exact hypothesis. H is a genuine remaining assumption and is NOT proved here.

Under H, the conclusion is that every Run true structuralEdge k (retRaw 10) has
17 <= k. It is a conditional 17-operation bound, not the full posted statement
excluding all circuits of at most 19 gates that contain a NOT.

Retention and budget review
The raw-input list is nodup because distinct projections are separated by a basis
assignment. The first Run decomposition therefore supplies distinct p and q and a
rest list independent of both. first_keeps_left forces the original left operand
to survive the first operation. The continuation state is thus the combined wire,
the retained left input, and optional other original inputs.

After restricting p false, the whole continuation state is available from the nine
raw roots using zero preprocessing gates. The retained left input is a constant,
so its cost is exactly 1 and the continuation state's mass is at least 1. The copied
projected_bound lemma then gives

  15 + mass(projected continuation state) <= continuation steps.

Thus continuation steps >= 16, and restoring the initial operation gives at least
17 steps. There is no double counting: the initial operation is outside the Run
continuation to which projected_bound is applied; the one unit of mass belongs to
the explicitly retained original input. The combined wire's possible additional
constant cost is not needed for the stated 17 bound. The nonconstant projected goal
is proved separately, as required by contraction/projected_bound.
