### Reader counting follow-up
For a trimmed circuit with B binary gates, U unary NOT gates, I edges from input vertices, and F total excess internal fanout (beyond one outgoing edge at every non-output internal gate), edge counting gives `2B+U = I+(B+U-1)+F`, hence total gates `S = I+F+U-1`. At S<=24, this gives I+F+U<=25 and at least three input vertices with one reader.

A CONDITIONAL census was tested: assume the read-once set R is independent and each vertex outside R has at least `max(2, |N(v) intersect R|)` input occurrences. Under those UNPROVED assumptions, minimum I for |R|=3,4,5,6,7 is 25,24,23,23,26. This would force F+U<=2 in a 24-gate candidate. These assumptions are not established for circuits with internal sharing/negation; a compensated version might be the needed stronger lemma.

Concrete obstacle: generic Pays permits a degree-three variable with exactly two OR readers, paying false2/true4. Eliminating that variable yields only a23-gate ceiling even if its residual lower bounds meet the known star upper bounds. Proving that such a two-OR-reader pattern either incurs additional internal-sharing/NOT cost or forces a more expensive residual is the missing structural step. No claimed theorem or Lean result from this strategy yet.
