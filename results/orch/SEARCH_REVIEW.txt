MILP and SAT models

- Distinct ordered predecessors remove only commutativity and idempotent redundancy. Every non-output gate being used later is valid for a minimum circuit; in the acyclic model this forces every gate to reach the final output.
- Searching exactly s gates can support a lower bound through s: any smaller nonconstant circuit can be padded one gate at a time by applying its final operation to its output and one of that final gate's operands, preserving the function and predecessor distinctness.

Petersen representatives [0,1] and [0,7] represent intersecting and disjoint 2-subsets respectively. Completeness of representation:
- Order the original OR gates topologically. Each operand's intervening AND-only region flattens, by associativity and idempotence, into a conjunction of input variables, earlier OR outputs, and constants. Sharing or duplicating AND-only regions affects no charged OR count.
- Conversely, every selected conjunction is realizable by a finite fan-in-two AND circuit with unrestricted AND count, and each modeled OR is one binary OR gate. The final conjunction adds no OR gate.
- Empty conjunction represents true (a permitted constant AND in the public circuit model); the explicit false source represents false. A singleton conjunction represents an input or earlier OR wire. Thus constants and direct/unused outputs are covered.
- The model permits unused OR gates. A circuit with fewer than k OR gates can be padded with unused ORs, so exactly k modeled OR nodes covers all circuits with at most k OR gates.
- Ordering the selection bitmasks of the two operands imposes only OR commutativity. No graph-specific symmetry or mandatory-use restriction removes additional possibilities.

Constraint audit:
- For an earlier OR source q, auxiliary bad is exactly selected AND NOT q, enforced by the three binary inequalities.
- For each sample, meet is true exactly when no selected source is false; the upper bounds and meet+sum(bad)>=1 enforce both directions, including empty conjunctions and selected true inputs.
- OR output constraints enforce the binary OR truth table. All modeled variables have binary integrality and bounds.
- Independently enumerated all local truth assignments for the bad-variable constraints, meet constraints with zero through four blockers, and OR-output constraints. Every feasible assignment matches the intended Boolean function.

Samples and witness scope:
- In the default mode, edge-pair positives and maximal-independent-set negatives characterize the entire monotone graph function. Returned witnesses are additionally evaluated on the entire Boolean cube.

A SAT witness in this mode is not a full graph-function circuit unless separately checked on all inputs. UNSAT for this relaxed model would imply UNSAT for the full model, still subject to solver trust.
- Any claimed lower bound concerns only OR count, with arbitrary AND count. This is not yet a lower bound on total gate count or a Lean certificate.

Safety and numerical limitations:
- No credential/environment access, network clients, subprocess calls, external mutations, or file writes occur in the reviewed script.
