# Completed Alternative6 partial-test refinement trials

These sources adapt the reviewed Triangle9 CEGIS workflow to the exact19-input24-edge Alternative6 graph. The target is28 gates; every decoded SAT model is replayed on all88 raw positive/maximal-negative assignments before it can be called a computational witness. All saved candidate models and both neighborhood seeds are explicitly INVALID. The known29gate zero-edit calibration decoded exactly and passed all88 cases; full calibration and independent source reviews are preserved.

Run001 used32selected tests (all24positive plus8negative cases covering every nonedge pair),8edits around the28gate mixed near4 seed,120seconds per solver call. Round0 timed out with no verdict. Run002 used25selected tests (all24positive plus the all-right negative), the same8edit seed and120seconds. Round0 was SAT but failed48raw cases; adding12negative cases produced37selected tests, and round1 timed out. Pair coverage is never treated as complete verification.

Run003 used the reviewed Gradual variant,25initial tests,8edits around the27gate multi-dirty seed padded to28,60seconds per round,24round limit, and only2new failed negative cases per refinement. SAT models on25/27/29tests failed56/32/32raw cases respectively. At31tests round3 timed out. No target witness or UNSAT result is claimed for these three runs. Every neighborhood remains centered on the original seed. Complete generation/solver/main stdout and stderr, exact selected tests and invalid models are preserved; generated CNFs and maps are reproducible and omitted.

Reproduce with SearchCegisAlternative6.py (runs001/002) or SearchCegisAlternative6Gradual.py (run003), passing --solver <cadical-path> --statement statement.txt --tests <the-listed-test-file> --nearby <the-listed-INVALID-seed> --output-dir <scratch-directory> --edits 8 --round-seconds <120or60> --rounds 24 and, for the Gradual source, --refine-count 2. The option names and values must be separate shell arguments.
