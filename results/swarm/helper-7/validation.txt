Encoding and validation:
- Positive rows are graph edges. Negative rows are maximal independent sets.
  For monotone circuits these boundary rows determine the complete graph function.
- Every gate selects two earlier wires and AND or OR. A real prefix followed by
  repeated-input copy gates represents at most k gates, not only exactly k.
- Normalization removes redundant gates on the boundary rows, enforces useful gates,
  retains all live gates, and orders gates by maximum predecessor index.
- Optional symmetry uses explicitly verified graph automorphisms and colexicographic
  pair representatives compatible with that gate order.
- Optional dominance and oriented-operand pruning follow from replacing a gate by
  an earlier wire that improves every specified row toward the required output.
