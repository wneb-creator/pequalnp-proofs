usage: prepare_positive_repairs.py [-h] [--budgets [BUDGETS ...]]
                                   [--local-counts [LOCAL_COUNTS ...]]
                                   [--max-false-positive-rows MAX_FALSE_POSITIVE_ROWS]
                                   [--analysis-only]
                                   source output

Analyze all-positive near-circuits and prepare exact-target repairs; no
solver. The source may have false positives, but all 16 target edges must be
accepted. Every generated formula retains all target boundary constraints.
Output is free; there is no first-junk, second-junk, clean-prefix, liveness or
tail restriction.

positional arguments:
  source
  output

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --budgets [BUDGETS ...]
  --local-counts [LOCAL_COUNTS ...]
  --max-false-positive-rows MAX_FALSE_POSITIVE_ROWS
  --analysis-only
