Primary literature read on 2026-11-23:
- Stasys Jukna, Boolean Function Complexity, Chapter 9, section 9.9:
  https://web.vu.lt/mif/s.jukna/boolean/Chapter9.pdf
  Short quotation: "just one NOT gate be used at the top of the circuit!"
  The surrounding argument separates selected positive and negative test inputs
  with a negated threshold. It does not prove or disprove the posted statement.
- Sajin Koroth and Jayalal Sarma, Depth Lower Bounds against Circuits with
  Sparse Orientation, Remark 3:
  https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2014/072/download/
  It establishes a depth separation for some monotone functions with one NOT.
  Therefore an unrestricted monotone-function elimination theorem cannot be
  assumed. The special structure of the OR outputs is essential here.
